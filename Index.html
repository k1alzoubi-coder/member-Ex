    Why these metrics

    NPS. Asked on day 30, when a member can first judge the relationship rather than one transaction, and 75 days before renewal, early enough to win back a detractor before the decision. The two readings per member show how the year changed the relationship.

    CES. Health declaration, identity check, claim submission, booking, policy changes and renewal. Effort predicts whether a member abandons the task or calls instead, which satisfaction does not. Asked on completion, and for identity checks only after a struggle.

    CSAT. Calls, chats, the welcome call, telehealth, clinic visits, claim decisions and closed complaints all have a clear end and a person or outcome to judge. One driver question after a clinic visit shows which part of the visit to fix.

    Task success and exit reason. "Did you find what you were looking for?" on the plan, network and benefit pages separates a content gap from a product gap. "What stopped you today?" on the price page, and the reason for leaving at cancellation, go straight into root-cause review.

      One member, every source

      • Start from the member ID. Matching by phone number pulls in hospitals and employers that call often.
      • Record how each match was made, and how confident it is.
      • Sentiment joins on the call or chat, then on the member.
      • Agents see only what they need. Health data stays in the protected analytics layer, as the PDPL requires.
      • One clock: Saudi time, working days Sunday to Thursday.
      How the joins work

      Member first. Every record carries the member ID or is resolved to it from the member's side. Matching phone numbers the other way round brings in hospitals, employers and family members who call often and look like heavy users.

      Honest matches. Where a record cannot be tied with certainty, such as a social media post or a call from an unknown number, we store the match method and a confidence level, and report coverage openly instead of assuming it.

      Sentiment. Speech and text analytics score each call and chat. The score joins on contact ID and then member ID, so it can sit beside the claim, the survey answer and the renewal outcome.

      Two layers. Operational tools such as the agent desktop and the ticket hold identifiers and what the agent needs to act. Clinical and financial context lives in the analytics layer with role-based access. Health data is sensitive personal data under the PDPL, so access is limited to what each role needs.
